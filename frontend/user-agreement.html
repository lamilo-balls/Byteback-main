User Agreement

Acknowledgement required before filing a complaint

1. Purpose

This User Agreement governs your use of the complaint filing and tracking features of the ByteBack portal.

2. Cooperation with Investigators

You agree to respond to reasonable requests for additional information from assigned investigators to support the resolution of your complaint.

3. Evidence Submission

Any evidence you upload must be your own or lawfully obtained, and relevant to the incident being reported.

4. Confidentiality

Details of your case are treated as confidential and shared only with authorized personnel involved in the investigation.
