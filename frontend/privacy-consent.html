Data Privacy Consent

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

1. Collection of Personal Information

ByteBack collects personal information such as your name, contact details, address, and details of the incident you report, solely for the purpose of processing, investigating, and resolving cybercrime complaints.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your data will be used to verify your identity, coordinate with assigned investigators, communicate case updates, and support law enforcement action where necessary.

3. Data Sharing

Shared only with authorized law enforcement and cybercrime investigation units, and partner government agencies directly involved in your case. Never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Your Rights Under RA 10173

You have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to erasure or blocking, to damages, to file a complaint, and to data portability, as provided under the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

5. Data Retention & Security

All information is encrypted at rest and in transit, and retained only for as long as necessary to resolve your complaint and comply with legal obligations.
